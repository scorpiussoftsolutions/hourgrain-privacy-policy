Hourgrain privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Hourgrain is a puzzle game published by Scorpius Labs, operating under its Scorpius Soft Solutions brand. It is intended for children and older players. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

Age choice

On first launch, the game asks whether the player is under 13 or 13 or older. This choice is saved only on the device. The game does not ask for a name or date of birth. Third-party ads are disabled for players who select under 13. Ads are not initialized until a player who selects 13 or older has completed any required consent steps.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, unlocked levels, daily streak, glass skins) and your settings (sound, vibration) are saved only on your device. We do not run servers for the game, we do not ask for an account, and we cannot see this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The free version may show ads through Google AdMob. Ads appear only between levels or when you choose to watch one for a reward. They never appear while you are solving a level.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID
  • IP address, used to estimate general location
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics, such as crash and performance data

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ads are disabled for players who select under 13. For players who select 13 or older, the game uses Google's consent tools where required; ads are limited to a general-audience rating because children are part of the intended audience. If consent options are required, they are available in Settings › Privacy options. Android users can also reset or delete their advertising ID in device settings.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Vibration: for the short buzz when you flip a glass. You can turn it off in Settings.

The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

Hourgrain is intended for children and older players. The game itself does not require an account and does not ask for names, email addresses, or dates of birth. Progress and settings stay on the device. Third-party ads are disabled for players who select under 13. Google Mobile Ads is initialized only for players who select 13 or older and complete any required consent steps. Google may process the advertising and diagnostic data described above to provide the service.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
